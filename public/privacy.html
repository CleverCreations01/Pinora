PINVORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

PINVORA is a Pinterest content planning and creator productivity application operated by Clever Creations.

1. Information PINVORA processes

When you connect Pinterest, PINVORA uses Pinterest OAuth to receive the permissions you approve. Depending on enabled features, this may include board names and identifiers, Pin details and publishing responses. PINVORA does not request or collect your Pinterest password, session cookies or login credentials.

PINVORA may process content drafts, titles, descriptions, images or image URLs, affiliate links and preferences that you enter or create in the app. Workspace drafts and preferences are currently stored in your browser on your device.

2. How information is used

Information is used to connect your account, display boards, test Pinterest API functionality, create or manage Pins when authorised, maintain your workspace and troubleshoot the service. PINVORA does not sell personal information.

3. Credentials and security

Pinterest OAuth tokens are handled server-side and stored in encrypted form in private server storage. Application secrets are kept in server environment variables and are not sent to the browser. No online service can guarantee absolute security; users should never share passwords, API secrets or one-time codes.

4. Third-party services

PINVORA communicates with Pinterest when you connect your account or use Pinterest features. Future optional features may connect to affiliate networks, product-data providers or AI providers. When enabled, only information needed for the requested feature should be sent to those providers, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Data retention and deletion

Browser-stored workspace data remains on your device until you clear it or remove it. Server-side OAuth tokens are retained while the account connection is active so that authorised API actions can work. You may revoke PINVORA's access through Pinterest's account/app settings. For deletion requests relating to server-side connection data, contact clevercreations1902@gmail.com.

6. Children's privacy

PINVORA is not designed for children under 13.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as features and data practices change. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact clevercreations1902@gmail.com.
