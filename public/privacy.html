PINORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

PINORA is a Pinterest content planning and automation application operated by Clever Creations. This Privacy Policy explains what information PINORA may access and how it is used.

1. Information PINORA accesses

When you connect a Pinterest account, PINORA may access information that Pinterest authorizes through OAuth, including your Pinterest profile, boards, Pins, publishing-related information, and analytics needed for the features you enable.

PINORA does not ask for or collect your Pinterest password, session cookies, or other Pinterest login credentials.

2. How information is used

PINORA uses authorized Pinterest information to help you plan, create, optimize, schedule, publish, and analyze Pinterest content. Information is used only to provide the Pinterest-related services you request. PINORA does not use Pinterest API data to train, fine-tune, or improve AI or machine-learning models.

3. Third-party services

PINORA may use third-party services such as Pinterest and AI or product-data providers to provide features that you explicitly enable. Data sent to a third-party service is limited to what is necessary for that feature and is handled according to that provider's policies. PINORA does not sell or provide Pinterest API data to unrelated third parties.

4. Credentials and security

PINORA is designed to use Pinterest OAuth authorization. Pinterest access credentials and application secrets should be stored securely and are not intended to be exposed in the app interface or source code.

5. Data sharing

PINORA does not sell your personal information. PINORA does not intentionally share your Pinterest account information with unrelated third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Data retention and deletion

You may disconnect PINORA from your Pinterest account at any time through the relevant Pinterest account/app authorization controls. PINORA is designed to request Pinterest data as needed rather than retain Pinterest API data unnecessarily. Where PINORA stores information you provide directly for the app's operation, you may request deletion of that information by contacting the app operator.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PINORA's features or integrations change. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact
